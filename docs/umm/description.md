# Umm

Pick a mystery card, share your answer, and discover how other people think. Umm is a playful question-card game with a painted meadow world, customizable cards and gentle piano sounds. Coins reward participation, not correct answers.

## Free features

- Discover player-made questions first, then ready-made mysteries from Umm.
- Answer with choices, text, or numbered and bullet lists. Press Enter for another item and drag to reorder.
- Create a single question; add another to turn it into a quiz of up to 20 questions.
- Choose timers from 15 seconds to 10 minutes, or allow unlimited time.
- Let submitted answers grow through additions while protecting the original text and list items, or make answers final.
- Design cards with palettes, stickers, textures and motion; read response boards, vote and revisit your history.
- Use on-device dictation on supported Android 12+ phones. Adjust or mute interface sounds in Settings.

## PRO Features

* AI assist drafts questions from a topic or supplied text.
* AI remix reworks a question from your instruction.
* AI palette prompts suggest a named card look.

AI creator tools require Pro access for your RykerSoft account. Playing, manual creation and Android on-device dictation stay free. Open Settings → RykerSoft Pro to sign into the hub account; your Umm game account remains separate. Provider keys are managed by the service, not entered into the app.

## Accounts and connection

Google sign-in and a unique username are required to play. Questions, answers and coins are stored online. Cached browsing and supported on-device dictation work offline, but saving and submitting answers require a connection. Recognition availability depends on Android and installed speech languages; unsupported phones can still use typed input or their keyboard microphone. Browser cloud dictation is not configured in this release.
