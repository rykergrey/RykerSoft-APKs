# MechaMeme

Formerly Meme God, MechaMeme turns your photos into editable memes in a playful, image-first Android workspace. Swipe between photos and takes, choose a classic meme style, or add your own direction.

- Import camera or gallery photos and crop them locally.
- Edit captions, typography and placement.
- Keep versions on your device and export or share PNGs.
- Pinch the framed image to zoom in place, then drag to pan.
- Create and customize your own meme templates.

## PRO Features

* Generate AI captions and revise them with a short direction.
* Recreate a photo as a new humorous scene.
* Optimize template instructions and generate example captions or images.

Sign in with the same Google account you use for RykerSoft. A Pro grant in any RykerSoft app unlocks MechaMeme's AI features. Keys and requests are handled automatically; no PC or API-key setup is needed. Free features remain available without Pro or sign-in.

Photos and drafts are stored on the device. AI actions send selected photos and instructions directly to the chosen provider. Generated personas and replies are fictional; MechaMeme does not post to social networks.
