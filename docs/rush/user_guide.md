Rush is an unofficial, offline-first companion for exploring the band's catalog and building a personal collection.

## 1. Start Exploring

Open the Home screen to use the featured spotlight and shortcuts into the discography, collection, timeline, band history, search, random songs, and random videos. The catalog is bundled with the application, so ordinary browsing works offline.

## 2. Browse Albums and Songs

Use Discography filters to browse studio, live, and solo releases or group albums by decade. Open an album for its overview, tracks, credits, liner notes, awards, artwork, and available video links. Open a song to read lyrics, see where it appears, and review its videos.

Long-press a track for quick collection actions, playback through an available external player, or an external search. Where both covers are available, use the artwork control to flip between front and back images.

## 3. Use Videos and External Links

Album and song video tabs organize official and user-added YouTube links. Choosing a video or a buy/stream link leaves Rush and opens the appropriate external application or browser. Internet access is required only for that external content.

## 4. Build My Collection

Pin albums, songs, covers, lyric excerpts, and other items you want to revisit. Add ratings, personal notes, selected lyric quotes, and custom YouTube links. My Collection can be searched and filtered without changing the bundled source catalog.

## 5. Export and Import

Export My Collection to a JSON backup before moving devices or making major changes. During import:

- **Merge** adds imported pins, quotes, notes, links, and ratings while keeping existing collection data.
- **Replace** clears the current collection and uses the imported backup instead.

Review the choice carefully before confirming Replace. Keep exported files somewhere you trust; they can contain personal notes and collection history.

## 6. Search, Timeline, and The Band

Search finds albums and songs by title or available lyric content. Timeline presents the studio catalog chronologically. The Band section provides history and pages for Geddy Lee, Alex Lifeson, and Neil Peart. Random Song and Random Video offer quick discovery when you do not have a specific destination in mind.

## 7. Data and Privacy

Rush does not require a RykerSoft account. Its bundled catalog and personal collection work locally, and collection data stays on the device unless you explicitly export it. The application is unofficial and is not affiliated with Rush or Anthem Entertainment.
