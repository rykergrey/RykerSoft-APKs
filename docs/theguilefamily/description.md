# The Guile Family

A painted party game for one shared Android phone. Play as Ryker, Nemo and Ender in three distinct games: spot the Bluffer, act out mysterious Party Guests, or race through the same twelve cards in Same Again.

Pass the phone for private Hold to peek cards, then enjoy a character finale based on the actual outcome. Build your own source packs in the public Content Workshop, save them locally, and exchange them as files or text. The complete game works offline.

[PRO Features](#pro-features)

## PRO Features

* **AI pack drafts and remixes** for all three games, using public inspiration and source cards.
* **Party Guests hint coaching** that preserves identities and their order.
* **Party Guests setting remix** before private dealing, preserving the selected identities.

Open Settings, sign in with the same Google account used in RykerSoft, and ask the RykerSoft administrator to enable Pro Access specifically for The Guile Family. An existing grant for another app does not unlock this app. Choose Refresh after access is changed. AI also needs an internet connection and a configured provider. Sign out or disable AI in Settings whenever you prefer. All game modes, writing, starters, saved packs and pack exchange remain available offline without Pro.
