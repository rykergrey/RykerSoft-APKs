## Start a game

Choose a mode and select the participating character cards. Bluffer always uses Ryker, Nemo and Ender; Party Guests and Same Again use two or three of them. Players use their character's name. Read the public briefing and pass the phone to the named holder. Hold to peek; release to hide. Every private screen shows its holder's name and portrait.

## Three games

- **Bluffer:** everyone except the Bluffer knows the word. Give clues, then each player privately votes for another player. Tap to reveal votes received. A unique plurality accuses; a tie or wrong suspect means the Bluffer wins. A caught Bluffer privately gets up to two different final guesses. A correct guess wins for the Bluffer; otherwise the group wins. Individual votes become public only after the result.
- **Party Guests:** the rotating Host welcomes the other players, who act as secret identities. Guess through three acts. Guess every identity to win together; otherwise everyone retries.
- **Same Again:** each player gets one 30-second turn per act, with a fresh shuffled copy of the same twelve targets. Other players guess. Describe, give one-word clues, then mime. Correct answers rank first; shorter active time breaks equal counts. Exact ties share wins, and an all-zero round has no winner. Peek and hide the current card before Got it or Pass; the next card starts sealed.

## Content Workshop

Write a pack or choose an offline starter. Bluffer needs eight distinct comparable words and a category; Party Guests needs two to twelve identities, two hints each, and a public setting and name that do not identify a guest; Same Again needs twelve distinct targets. AI drafts six Guest identities. Review every draft and explicitly save before play. A Guest pack author knows the identity pool: let someone who has not read it host, or use fresh built-in content.

Your packs holds up to forty saved source packs. Use, edit, confirm removal or undo the last removal. Share a saved pack, export a .somethingsoff.json file, or copy its text. Import Pack File and Review Pasted Pack open an unsaved draft for review; Save creates a new local copy. Files contain one validated source pack, never a live round, and accept up to 32 KiB UTF-8. The chosen sharing app controls delivery. There is no cloud library synchronization.

## PRO Features

* **AI pack drafts and remixes** for all three games, using public inspiration and source cards.
* **Party Guests hint coaching** that preserves identities and their order.
* **Party Guests setting remix** before private dealing, preserving the selected identities.

Open Settings, sign in with the same Google account used in RykerSoft, and ask the RykerSoft administrator to enable Pro Access specifically for The Guile Family. An existing grant for another app does not unlock this app. Choose Refresh after access is changed. AI also needs an internet connection and a configured provider. Sign out or disable AI in Settings whenever you prefer. All game modes, writing, starters, saved packs and pack exchange remain available offline without Pro.

## Privacy and interruptions

AI requests occur only after an explicit draft, remix, coaching or setting action. Public inspiration and source content may be sent to OpenAI. Dealt secrets, ballots, guesses, rosters, scores and round progress are excluded. The app retains no audio. Optional dictation may use the system speech provider; typed input remains available.

Release, backgrounding, settings and Escape hide private screens. Backgrounding pauses active timers and cancels AI and speech. Resume explicitly when required. File-picker callbacks survive normal picker backgrounding, but navigation or settings discard late file results. Ballots and round data remain in memory. Private Android screens and app-switcher thumbnails are protected from screenshots.
