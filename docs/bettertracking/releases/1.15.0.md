# Better Tracking 1.15.0

November 10, 2026 · Android versionCode 31

Chat journal proposals for saved library items now show **Log**, **Tray** and Cancel in place of Apply. Log saves the entry and Tray moves it to the staging tray; both apply in place without the log impact tally, and the card then reads “Logged to …” or “Added to tray”. A proposal that is ignored saves nothing. Estimated one-off meals keep a single **Log meal** button because they are not library items and cannot be staged.

New chat entries go to the day selected in the journal, at the current clock time, unless the request names a day or time. The app applies that default itself whenever the assistant supplies no date. Every pending log card states the date and time it will be logged to.

A proposal sent to the tray keeps its date as the item's own when that date is more than 15 minutes from the present, such as a backdated day. A proposal for the present follows the tray date. Staging the first item still returns the journal to today.

Each staging tray row has a date next to its time. Setting it gives that item its own date; items with their own date are highlighted and can be returned to the tray date from the expanded row. The tray date and time continue to apply to every item without its own, and the footer reports how many that is. Duplicating an item creates a copy that follows the tray date until it is given its own. Stages created through the Index Assistant connection keep their saved date.

The journal's selected-items panel now starts with its list collapsed behind a “Selected (n)” toggle, and clear buttons in search fields have a larger touch target.

Available as a production-signed Android APK, Windows x64 portable EXE, Linux x64 AppImage and Debian package. Windows executables are not Authenticode signed. Automated tests, type checks and the production build cover this release; the new chat and tray behavior was not exercised on a physical device before publication.
