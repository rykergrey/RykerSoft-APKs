# bettertracking 1.11.0

October 13, 2026 · Android versionCode 23

The Analysis tab now turns the six-domain journal into day, week, month, year, all-time and custom health reports. Explore interactive numeric trends, nutrient contributors and goal rules, training and body measurements, habit distributions, notes and previous-period comparisons. Daily/domain coverage keeps missing data visible, and selected dates open their journal records.

Relationships compare same-day, next-day or two-days-later observations with scatter plots and descriptive correlations when at least eight complete date pairs vary. These are associations in recorded data, not evidence of causation. Reports support up to 3,660 days per range; older history can be explored in smaller periods.

Health Coach reviews use the same calculated reports with saved goals and constraints. Choose an in-app review, a focused follow-up or a portable brief for your own chatbot. Charts and CSV / Markdown exports work without an AI request. Phones use the compact Explore selector to move among sections.

This release also includes the logging tally introduced in 1.10.0 and refined in 1.10.1: before values, combined batch contributions, new totals and a separate Goal adjustments section. Tray/combo batches and one-off meals save together, including mixed food/exercise logs. Pause, skip, persistent results and reduced-motion support make the tally controllable.

Available packages: signed Android APK, Windows x64 portable EXE, Linux x64 AppImage and Debian package. The Windows portable build is not Authenticode signed. In-app AI uses the configured provider credentials; portable briefs use the external chatbot's account.
