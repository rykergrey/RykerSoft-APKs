# Better Tracking 1.12.1

October 20, 2026 · Android versionCode 27

Improves the [Index Assistant connection](../index-assistant-bridge.md) by delivering a native queue notification to the already loaded owning runtime. It does not open a new activity/WebView or create authority to execute while signed out, bootstrapping, expired, cancelled or revoked. Cold runtimes retain the explicit Open app handoff.

Wake bursts and requests arriving during an operation share one serialized drain; all native queue pages can finish without relying on Android background interval timers. Native wakes renew the readiness heartbeat. Interval polling and canonical receipt reconciliation remain fallbacks. Disposal removes the listener, including an outstanding listener registration.

Verification covers immediate wake without timers, coalescing during an awaited operation, multiple queue pages, ownership/readiness loss, listener cleanup and registration failure. Release artifacts and local verification records are saved separately. Publication is not claimed.
