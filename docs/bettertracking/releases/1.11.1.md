# bettertracking 1.11.1

October 14, 2026 · Android versionCode 24

On Home, choose W for a week or M for a month. Trends & History brings all programmatic metrics, charts, relationships and food exploration together with the existing filtered log items. Its six statistics sections use the phone Explore selector and the app's existing style. Nutrition and weight progress come first, with calorie/protein/fiber/weight comparisons and a dedicated recorded weight trend.

Open the separate Analysis tab, or choose Coach review, for AI analysis of the same selected period. Reviews generate inline and save weekly/monthly reports and findings to the existing account analysis storage. Exports and chat follow-ups keep that period's context. The default review prioritizes nutrition and confirmed weight goals, using calculated values, comparisons, coverage and saved constraints. Training, habits, relationships and food exploration remain available in Trends & History.

Available packages: signed Android APK, Windows x64 portable EXE, Linux x64 AppImage and Debian package. The Windows portable build is not Authenticode signed. Statistics and data exports are calculated locally; in-app AI generation uses configured provider credentials. No new backend migration is required.
