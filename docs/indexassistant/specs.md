# Index Assistant requirements

- Android 10 (API 29) or later
- Package `com.rykersoft.indexassistant`
- Internet connection for AI answers, transcription and voices
- Optional: Index 01 ring with the Pebble app set to send hold gestures to Index's local webhook
- Optional: Pebble watch for reading replies (the watch app installs from Index)
- Optional: Google account for Calendar, Tasks, Gmail, Drive and Sheets, connected per service in Connections
- Optional: Bluetooth microphone or headset
- AI providers: your own Gemini, OpenAI, Groq or ElevenLabs keys, or RykerSoft Pro
- Permissions: microphone, notifications, exact alarms (for reminders), and optional Bluetooth and notification access
