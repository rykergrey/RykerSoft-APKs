## Getting started

- Open Index Assistant, then on the Providers page either sign in to RykerSoft Pro with Google or add your own provider keys.
- Allow microphone and notification access when asked. For reminders, allow Alarms & reminders.
- Operator is ready from the start. Recruit specialists from Crew → Recruit.

## Talking to your crew

- Hold the talk key, speak, and release to send. Turn on Edit before sending to dictate a draft first.
- Say a contact's name to reach them, or let Operator route you. Type @ to mention a contact.
- Replies are spoken in the contact's voice. Triple press the ring, or tap the portrait, to replay or stop.
- Say "that's all for tonight" or "log this" and the contact writes a logbook entry.

## Reminders and alarms

- Say "remind me in an hour to check the oven". It is set at once and confirmed with its time.
- When it is due, the contact rings like a phone call: Dismiss, Call back later, or Answer to talk.
- Everything held is listed on the Intel page; set an alarm by hand with + ALARM.

## Google services

- Connect each service under Connections, then choose which contacts may use it under Agent tool access.
- Ask in your own words: "add groceries to my Family calendar on the 15th, all day". It is added at once and the reply says exactly what was saved.
- Event cards have Open in Calendar and Edit details. Change the calendar, title, all-day, date and time, location or notes, then Save changes. You can also reply "make it 3pm".
- To use calendars other than your primary one, reconnect Calendar once.
- Email drafts are saved for you, but only your tap on Send email sends them.

## Ring and watch

- In Settings → Ring controls choose what Hold and Tap-then-hold do.
- In the Pebble app set both hold gestures to Webhook only, using `http://127.0.0.1:18765/index/hold` and `http://127.0.0.1:18765/index/double-hold`.
- Install the watch reader from Settings to read replies on your Pebble.

## PRO Features

* Access is managed for your RykerSoft account. Sign in with the same Google account in Index Assistant (Providers page, RykerSoft Pro) and Pro supplies AI answers, transcription and voices.
* Pro keys stay in memory only and are never written to the phone.
* Your own provider keys still work and take priority.
