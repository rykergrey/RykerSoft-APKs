# Index Assistant

Index Assistant is a voice communicator for Android built around a crew of AI contacts. Talk to them by holding the Index 01 ring, a Bluetooth microphone or the phone's push-to-talk key, and the right contact answers in its own voice, on a codec-style screen, on your Pebble watch, or both. Operator is the general contact who keeps your reminders and routes you to specialists you recruit, each with its own role, voice, model and memory.

Contacts can work with your Google Calendar, Tasks, Gmail, Drive and Sheets, set alarms that ring like a phone call, keep logbooks and standing orders, and connect to your other RykerSoft apps (Hyperscribe Mobile, SuperThinking, Better Tracking) when you allow it.

## Features

- Hold-to-talk from the Index 01 ring, a Bluetooth mic or the phone, with every recording saved before it is answered
- A crew of contacts: Operator plus specialists you recruit, each with its own voice, model, instructions and memory
- Codec-style conversations with spoken replies, replay and a watch reader
- Reminders and alarms that ring as an incoming call from the contact who set them
- Google Calendar, Tasks, Gmail, Drive and Sheets: events are added when you ask and shown with every detail, an Open in Calendar link and Edit details
- Anything that reaches another person, such as sending an email, waits for your tap on Send
- Logbooks, standing orders, check-ins and an Intel page listing everything the crew holds

## PRO Features

* AI answers, transcription and voices without your own provider keys: RykerSoft Pro supplies them for the RykerSoft account you sign in with in Index Assistant
* Your own provider keys remain supported and take priority over Pro keys
