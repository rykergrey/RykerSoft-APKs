# Earbound Updates

## v0.9.0

- The circle of fifths: a new touch wheel you answer by pressing, sweeping to a key and lifting. Each key sounds as you pass it, and lifting in the centre cancels
- Five new exercises in a new practice group, Keys & the circle: Build the circle, Key signatures, Inside a key, Trace the path and Circle moves. They cover fifths up and down, reading and counting signatures, relative and parallel minors, the six chords of a key, modes, cadences and falling chains you hear, dominants, the tritone swap and transposing instruments
- The circle is part of every campaign sector and gets harder from sector to sector. In sector VIII the wheel is blank: only C is fixed at the top
- Eleven new lessons on the circle, from Up a fifth to Dominants and far keys, each with a playable wheel
- 26 glossary terms with playable wheel figures, each linked to its lesson where there is one
- Eight new achievements, recorded separately for knowledge and for ear tasks
- Party: the host can choose any circle exercise. Rounds you read open for answers at once; rounds you hear wait for the host to play them
- Sharps and flats are now engraved glyphs everywhere, instead of the letters "#" and "b"
- Home's Continue opens the route choice when more than one route is open
- Voice answers accept a key named by its letter alone, such as "E flat"

## v0.8.3

- Campaign: after a cleared match, choose your next match right on the Results screen. Each choice shows what it is worth and why, such as "62% · last 3 visits"
- An exercise you have missed more on recently, or have not tried yet, is worth up to one and a half times the points, and the experience you earn grows by the same amount. The value is set when the match starts and never changes afterwards
- The sector map shows the same values on each route
- Windows and Linux are updated to this version, including the lessons, staff and Results improvements from 0.8.1 and 0.8.2

## v0.8.2

- Recall and reading: unanswered notes on the staff are now faint vertical lines instead of dots on the middle line, so they no longer look like B notes. The next note to enter glows gold
- Results: the Replay and Next match buttons are drawn in full, with their glow no longer cut off

## v0.8.1

- Listening: a small spark now travels from each note to the next as they sound, landing on the note as you hear it, and follows the notes if the layout shifts. Chords have no spark, and reduced motion turns it off
- Reading: notes on the staff no longer slide, bounce or ripple. Each note sits on its exact line or space from the first frame, and colour and a soft column of light show your answer
- Stopping, replaying or leaving the app mid-sound now clears the glow cleanly

## v0.8.0

- Lessons: 28 short, hands-on lessons in nine topics, from how a match works to seventh chords, modes, reading and rhythm. Hear examples on a lit keyboard, compare two sounds, build shapes on the keys and answer quick checks
- Every lesson ends with three practice matches, Warm-up, Steady and Stretch, open to everyone; the suggested one follows your recorded accuracy
- Lesson suggestions on Home, the Lessons page and after a set, each with the reason from your own records, such as two sounds you keep mixing up. Put one off for a week with a tap
- Hold any label, number or control for a short explanation (right-click or the ? key on a computer)
- Records has a Lessons section: lessons finished, time spent, checks answered, practice played, and your accuracy before and after a lesson
- Pass the phone, rebuilt: 2 to 8 players, any exercise at Gentle, Steady or Bold, 3 to 10 sounds each, pass after every sound or after a whole set, a hidden-till-the-end reveal, and the option for everyone to hear the same sounds
- Lesson history travels with backups

## v0.7.0

First RykerSoft release.

- New-player placement: up to three matches, with an “I don’t know” option, place you at the right campaign sector. Checkpoints need 5 of 6 correct across three skills, so lucky guesses cannot place you higher
- Practice exercises and settings now unlock through campaign progress and placement
- More than 100 achievements covering knowledge (reliable intervals, chord qualities, inversions, modes, progressions, reading and recall), improvement over time, precision, and steady practice, each showing the date earned
- Redesigned Records screen with achievements, roomier cards and clearer layout on phones
- Complete backup and restore now includes achievements, cosmetics, practice setups, saved sketches, notebook bookmarks and every preference
- Removed the daily étude and the Studies quick-start panel; the Resonance sky and Kalimba sound now unlock through play, and anyone who earned them through daily études keeps them
- Release signing for update continuity

Installing this release over an earlier development build requires uninstalling that build first. Export a backup in Settings before uninstalling, then import it after installing.
