# Earbound Updates

## v0.8.0

- Now on Windows and Linux. The computer can host a party for phones on the same Wi-Fi
- Lessons: 28 short, hands-on lessons in nine topics, from how a match works to seventh chords, modes, reading and rhythm. Hear examples on a lit keyboard, compare two sounds, build shapes on the keys and answer quick checks
- Every lesson ends with three practice matches, Warm-up, Steady and Stretch, open to everyone; the suggested one follows your recorded accuracy
- Lesson suggestions on Home, the Lessons page and after a set, each with the reason from your own records, such as two sounds you keep mixing up. Put one off for a week with a tap
- Hold any label, number or control for a short explanation (right-click or the ? key on a computer)
- Records has a Lessons section: lessons finished, time spent, checks answered, practice played, and your accuracy before and after a lesson
- Pass the phone, rebuilt: 2 to 8 players, any exercise at Gentle, Steady or Bold, 3 to 10 sounds each, pass after every sound or after a whole set, a hidden-till-the-end reveal, and the option for everyone to hear the same sounds
- Lesson history travels with backups

## v0.7.0

First RykerSoft release.

- New-player placement: up to three matches, with an “I don’t know” option, place you at the right campaign sector. Checkpoints need 5 of 6 correct across three skills, so lucky guesses cannot place you higher
- Practice exercises and settings now unlock through campaign progress and placement
- More than 100 achievements covering knowledge (reliable intervals, chord qualities, inversions, modes, progressions, reading and recall), improvement over time, precision, and steady practice, each showing the date earned
- Redesigned Records screen with achievements, roomier cards and clearer layout on phones
- Complete backup and restore now includes achievements, cosmetics, practice setups, saved sketches, notebook bookmarks and every preference
- Removed the daily étude and the Studies quick-start panel; the Resonance sky and Kalimba sound now unlock through play, and anyone who earned them through daily études keeps them
- Release signing for update continuity

Installing this release over an earlier development build requires uninstalling that build first. Export a backup in Settings before uninstalling, then import it after installing.
