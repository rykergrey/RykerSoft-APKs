## Getting Started

Open Earbound and you land on the home screen. The arc of stars at the top is your current campaign sector; each star is a match. The large button below continues the campaign. The menu below that leads to Lessons, Practice, Party, Records, Customize and the Glossary of musical terms.

Use headphones or a quiet room, and set your volume before you begin.

## Placement

New players see **Find your starting point** on the home screen.

- Placement has up to three matches of 12 questions: Foundations, Core listening and Advanced ear.
- Each match tests two sector checkpoints. A checkpoint passes with 5 of its 6 answers correct, including at least one correct answer for each of its three skills.
- Choose **I don’t know** whenever you are unsure. It counts as a miss, which keeps the placement accurate; guessing cannot place you higher.
- If you pass both checkpoints in a match, the next match is offered. You can stop at any point and start where you have placed.
- Choose **Start from the beginning** to skip placement entirely.

Placement only moves you forward. Anything you skipped is still available to practise.

## The Campaign

The campaign has eight sectors of 60 matches. Each match is a short set of questions; clear it with at least 70% correct on your first answers. Every sixth match is a checkpoint. After each match you can choose between up to three activities, and the campaign keeps your activities balanced so no topic is skipped indefinitely. Each choice shows what it is worth: 100 points for every correct first answer, times 1, 1.25 or 1.5. An exercise you have missed more on recently, or have not tried, is worth more, and experience grows by the same amount.

From match 9 the campaign also trains the circle of fifths. Press the wheel, sweep around it (each key sounds as you
cross it) and lift on your answer; lift in the middle to cancel. Pads and voice work too. From sector IV the wheel gains an inner ring of
minor keys, and two more exercises join: Inside a key (relative keys, a key's chords, modes) and Trace the path (hear
chords move, then find them). Sector VII adds Circle moves (dominants, tritone swaps, and the keys B♭, E♭ and F
instruments read), and sector VIII hides every name on the wheel. All five exercises are in Practice, Pass the
phone and the networked Party, with eleven lessons under Keys & the circle and a glossary section of the same name.
When more than one match is open, Continue on Home takes you to the route choice.

Clearing matches unlocks new practice exercises, higher difficulties, sky effects, palettes and instrument sounds.

## Answering Questions

1. Tap the top of the screen to listen. Tap again to replay; replays are counted but never penalised.
2. Choose your answer with the buttons, the piano keyboard, or your voice (where offered).
3. Your first answer is the one that counts. After answering you can hear the sound again or open **Learn**.

**Learn** explains the question you just answered: the answer named in full, the idea behind it, something to see and hear, what your choice actually was, and the lesson that teaches the two apart. Score symbols play as real sound. On a right answer you can mark **It was a guess**; the answer still counts and your records note it. **Next** is at the bottom of the sheet, so you can carry on without closing it. **How this exercise works**, at the bottom of the first round, explains the exercise as a whole.

Melody recall questions ask you to play the phrase back on the keyboard, including the first note and any repeats, in the same octave.

## Lessons

Lessons (from Home) holds 28 short lessons in nine topics: Start here, Notes & keys, Intervals, Chords, Scales & modes, Harmony & keys, Reading music, Rhythm, and Playing it back.

- A lesson is a handful of steps. Read a few lines, hear examples on a lit keyboard, compare two sounds, build the shape on the keys, and answer a quick check.
- Use **Continue** once a step is done, or **Skip** to move on. **Previous** goes back.
- Every lesson ends with three practice matches: **Warm-up**, **Steady** and **Stretch**. The one marked **Suggested** follows your recorded accuracy on those sounds. These matches count in your records like any set.
- Lesson checks are never recorded as match results.

Earbound suggests lessons from your own records and says why, for example when two sounds crossed several times in recent sets. Suggestions appear on Home, on the Lessons page, after a set, and from glossary terms and practice exercises. Tap the cross on the Home card to put a suggestion off for a week.

## Explanations on Hold

Press and hold any label, number or control for a short explanation; on a computer, right-click it or press the ? key. The explanation opens in the other half of the screen, with a sound to hear when one helps and a link to the lesson that goes further. Holding never presses the button. During a match the explanation stays silent.

## Practice Studio

Practice lets you train any exercise you have unlocked, at any difficulty you have reached.

- Choose an exercise, then a level from 1 to 10, or customise targets, register, direction, voicing, tempo and phrase length.
- Save setups you like and return to them later, or share a setup as text.
- Focus suggestions on the home and Records screens target sounds you have been confusing.

## Records and Achievements

**Where you stand** sorts your skills into Solid, Developing, Guessing range, Struggling and Too few answers. A skill is Solid only when even the low end of an honest range for your number of answers is 70% or better, over at least two days; answers you marked as guesses do not count. Below it: what is worth a look (such as a sound you keep missing after studying it), your practice, **Over time** (sets and accuracy by week or month), each area month by month, and every skill.

**What you stopped to learn** counts the questions you opened Learn on, after a miss or a right answer.

Records also shows your first-answer accuracy, practice volume, an activity calendar, accuracy trends, your strongest and weakest sounds, common mix-ups, and timing records for matching conditions.

The Achievements card lists every achievement by category, with the date you earned it and your progress towards the rest. Many achievements reward real skill: a sound becomes **reliable** when you answer 9 of your last 10 correctly without hints.

The Lessons section shows lessons finished, time spent, checks answered, practice matches played, explanations opened, and your first-answer accuracy on a lesson's sounds before and after you finished it.

## Customize

Choose a sky effect, a colour palette and the instrument sound used for questions. Locked items show what earns them.

## Friends

Friends (from Home) lets you share your stats. It needs a Google sign-in; everything else in Earbound works without one.

1. Sign in and pick a username. Only your username is ever shown; your Google name and email stay private.
2. Type someone's username or email under **Add someone** and tap **Share my stats**. They can now see your stats. You see theirs when they add you back.
3. **Find me by email** lets someone who knows your exact address find you. Turn it off at any time.

Everyone sharing with you appears together: who practiced this week, **Needs a look**, **Over time**, **Everyone** (a table you can sort by any column), each person by area, shared mix-ups and each area's strength. Tap a name for that person's Where you stand. Make private **groups**, such as a class, to see one group at a time; only you see your groups. **Stop sharing** ends someone's access at once, and **Hide** removes someone who shares with you from your lists. On a computer the view spreads over two columns.

## Party Play

- **Host a party:** connect every device to the same Wi-Fi, choose Party → Host a party, and share the QR invitation. Guests join in a browser. Keep the host app open; the host phone’s speaker plays for everyone.
- **Pass the phone:** 2–8 players share one device and one speaker, offline. Choose the exercise, a difficulty (Gentle, Steady or Bold) and 3 to 10 sounds each, then set the house rules:
  - **Every sound** passes the phone after each answer; **Whole set** lets each player finish all their sounds first.
  - **Hidden till the end** keeps scores secret for one big reveal; **After each answer** shows right or wrong straight away.
  - **Fresh for each player** deals everyone different sounds; **Same for everyone** makes a true head-to-head.

Party scores belong to the room and are not added to your solo records.

## Settings, Backup and Restore

Settings holds sound, volume, room acoustics, answer feedback and reduced motion.

Under **App & data**, **Export backup** saves everything: history, stats, achievements and their dates, unlocks, cosmetics, practice setups, saved sketches, notebook bookmarks and preferences. **Import backup** restores it on this or another device after showing you what it contains. Earbound restarts after a full restore.

Progress is stored on your device. Export a backup before clearing app data or uninstalling.

### Sync between devices

Under **App & data → Sync**, tap **Sign in with Google**. Your progress, stats, achievements, unlocks and settings are then kept in your account and merged with every other device signed in to it: sets from all of them are combined, and a setting you change on one reaches the others. Volume stays separate on each device. Everything keeps working offline and syncs when you are back online.

Earbound syncs when it opens, after each set and when you switch away. Changes from another device load the next time Earbound opens; **Sync now** loads them straight away and restarts Earbound. With sync on, **Clear progress** clears every synced device. **Sign out** stops syncing on this device and keeps its progress. **Delete cloud copy** deletes the synced copy and your account.

## Tips

- Listen before you answer, and use I don’t know rather than guessing; your records and suggestions are more useful when they are honest.
- When a suggestion names a mix-up, take its lesson, then play its suggested practice match.
- Short daily sessions build streaks and steady improvement better than occasional long ones.
- Use Practice to revisit earlier material at any time.
