## Getting Started

Open Earbound and you land on the home screen. The arc of stars at the top is your current campaign sector; each star is a match. The large button below continues the campaign. The menu below that leads to Practice, Party, Records, Customize and the Glossary of musical terms.

Use headphones or a quiet room, and set your volume before you begin.

## Placement

New players see **Find your starting point** on the home screen.

- Placement has up to three matches of 12 questions: Foundations, Core listening and Advanced ear.
- Each match tests two sector checkpoints. A checkpoint passes with 5 of its 6 answers correct, including at least one correct answer for each of its three skills.
- Choose **I don’t know** whenever you are unsure. It counts as a miss, which keeps the placement accurate; guessing cannot place you higher.
- If you pass both checkpoints in a match, the next match is offered. You can stop at any point and start where you have placed.
- Choose **Start from the beginning** to skip placement entirely.

Placement only moves you forward. Anything you skipped is still available to practise.

## The Campaign

The campaign has eight sectors of 60 matches. Each match is a short set of questions; clear it with at least 70% correct on your first answers. Every sixth match is a checkpoint. After each match you can choose between up to three activities, and the campaign keeps your activities balanced so no topic is skipped indefinitely.

Clearing matches unlocks new practice exercises, higher difficulties, sky effects, palettes and instrument sounds.

## Answering Questions

1. Tap the top of the screen to listen. Tap again to replay; replays are counted but never penalised.
2. Choose your answer with the buttons, the piano keyboard, or your voice (where offered).
3. Your first answer is the one that counts. After answering you can hear the sound again or open **Learn** to compare it with other answers.

Melody recall questions ask you to play the phrase back on the keyboard, including the first note and any repeats, in the same octave.

## Practice Studio

Practice lets you train any exercise you have unlocked, at any difficulty you have reached.

- Choose an exercise, then a level from 1 to 10, or customise targets, register, direction, voicing, tempo and phrase length.
- Save setups you like and return to them later, or share a setup as text.
- Focus suggestions on the home and Records screens target sounds you have been confusing.

## Records and Achievements

Records shows your first-answer accuracy, practice volume, an activity calendar, accuracy trends, your strongest and weakest sounds, common mix-ups, and timing records for matching conditions.

The Achievements card lists every achievement by category, with the date you earned it and your progress towards the rest. Many achievements reward real skill: a sound becomes **reliable** when you answer 9 of your last 10 correctly without hints.

## Customize

Choose a sky effect, a colour palette and the instrument sound used for questions. Locked items show what earns them.

## Party Play

- **Host a party:** connect every device to the same Wi-Fi, choose Party → Host a party, and share the QR invitation. Guests join in a browser. Keep the host app open; the host phone’s speaker plays for everyone.
- **Pass the phone:** 2–6 players share one device, offline.

Party scores belong to the room and are not added to your solo records.

## Settings, Backup and Restore

Settings holds sound, volume, room acoustics, answer feedback and reduced motion.

Under **App & data**, **Export backup** saves everything: history, stats, achievements and their dates, unlocks, cosmetics, practice setups, saved sketches, notebook bookmarks and preferences. **Import backup** restores it on this or another device after showing you what it contains. Earbound restarts after a full restore.

Progress is stored only on your device. Export a backup before clearing app data, uninstalling, or changing phones.

## Tips

- Listen before you answer, and use I don’t know rather than guessing; your records and suggestions are more useful when they are honest.
- Short daily sessions build streaks and steady improvement better than occasional long ones.
- Use Practice to revisit earlier material at any time.
