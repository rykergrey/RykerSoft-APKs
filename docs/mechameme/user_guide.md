## Home and Community

Home opens Community. Switch to **My Library** for your private photos and generations. Use the filter button beside the tabs for sorting; signed-in users can enable **Hide mine** in Community. The header hides as you scroll down and returns as you scroll up.

Tap the floating plus button to import from the gallery or camera. Drag that button to reposition it. In My Library, tap an image to open its controls or swipe horizontally across it to compare versions. Visual dots mark the current version; dates appear above the image on the right.

To publish, sign in with Google through Settings and tap the thumbs-up on a library image. Choose a unique public username in Settings first. This uploads a public snapshot attributed only to that username; your Google name and email stay private. To remove a post, use its trash-can button in Community or its picture view, then confirm **Delete post**. This works even after the local library copy is deleted. Sign in with the Google account that originally posted it. The active library thumbs-up can also remove a publication. Removing a post cannot recall copies already downloaded. Tap **Remix** in the feed or picture view to start your own variation.

Publishing automatically adds your thumbs-up, so the image starts with one vote. In Community, sign in with Google and tap a thumbs-up to add or remove your vote, including on your own post. Each account gets one vote per image. When the total reaches zero, the image disappears from Community; private library copies stay safe.

Tap a Community image to open picture view. Its compact username badge sits above the complete image, with Remix and other actions below. Pinch to zoom and use Reset zoom to return. Close with Android Back, Escape or by tapping outside the picture view.

Use the **Add to library** image-plus icon to save a private copy and keep browsing. The button has no visible text and changes to a checkmark once saved. No sign-in is needed; adding a copy neither upvotes nor publishes it. You can find it in My Library later.

## Remix another player’s image

Tap **Remix** beneath a Community post, or open the image and tap **Remix** there. The image opens directly in the studio as your own private copy. No sign-in, file download dialog, crop confirmation or manual library import is needed.

Tap the pencil to add your captions, or enter a new twist and generate a take. Captions and caption generation are free. **Recreate me** can generate a new scene with RykerSoft Pro. Existing text on a shared snapshot is part of its image, so it cannot be edited as separate captions.

Your remix stays in My Library and remembers the original post and its public username. It does not change or delete the other player’s post. To share your variation back to Community, sign in with Google, choose your username and tap its thumbs-up. Starting a remix never publishes it automatically.

## Create and edit

Choose a photo, adjust its crop, then tap **Use photo**. Generate a caption with the mint generation button; add an optional direction or choose a meme style. Captions, revisions and template assistance are free without sign-in or Pro.

Use the pencil to write or edit captions, typography and placement. Double-tap the editor preview to add another caption. **Save as new take** preserves the original. Leaving an unsaved manual draft discards it.

## Keep, save and share

The feed actions run left to right: Share, Save, Thumbs up, Keep. Keep is a private bookmark. Save creates a PNG through Android's file picker. Share sends the image file through Android's share sheet; it does not publish to Community. Captions are baked into exported images. The original remains in your library.

## PRO Features

* Choose **Recreate me** to generate a new scene from your photo.

Open **Settings → RykerSoft Pro → Sign in with Google** using your RykerSoft account. Any active RykerSoft Pro grant unlocks Recreate me. Tap **Refresh access** after access changes. Internet is needed for AI, Community and account verification. Provider configuration is managed automatically.

## Templates and navigation

Open the meme-style picker to browse, choose or create templates. Auto lets the AI choose a format. In the picture workspace, swipe left/right between takes and up/down between photos. Tap or pull down the take indicator to open the take drawer. Pinch to zoom and drag to pan. Android Back resets zoom or returns Home.

The Library grid shows original photos with take-count badges. Hold a photo to rename or delete it; deletion asks for confirmation. Photo names stay on-device.

## Storage and privacy

Photos, takes and template edits are stored on-device, shared by accounts using that app installation. They are not cloud-synced. Export important images before clearing data or changing devices.

Only an explicit public thumbs-up uploads a Community snapshot. Public posts remain until removed by their owning account; deleting the local photo does not unpublish it. AI processing separately sends selected content to the service/provider.

Closing the app during generation can lose an in-flight result. Stopping the wait may not stop an already-running provider request; retrying can cause another request. Provider configuration and billing are administered through RykerSoft.
