## Start with a photo

Tap the plus button to choose a camera or gallery photo. Adjust the crop, then tap **Use photo**. The sample photo can be explored before importing your own.

## Edit, keep and share

After importing a photo, tap the pencil to write your own captions. Select the upper or lower caption area to edit it, or double-tap the preview to add another caption. Tap **Save as new take** to keep your captioned version alongside the original. Manual captions are free and do not require AI generation. Leaving the editor before saving discards this new draft.

Use the pencil action to edit a completed take's captions, size, color or position. The bookmark keeps a version. Export saves a PNG; Share opens Android's sharing options. Your original remains available alongside its versions. When you delete the selected take, the next take moves into that slot and stays selected. Deleting the last take selects the previous one; deleting the only take shows the original photo.

## PRO Features

* Tap the mint generation button for an AI caption. Add an optional direction to guide the joke.
* Choose **Recreate me** to generate a new scene based on the photo.
* Select a completed take and enter a direction to revise its captions.
* Use template AI assistance to refine instructions or create examples.

Open **Settings → RykerSoft Pro → Sign in with Google**. Use the same account as RykerSoft. Any active Pro grant in RykerSoft unlocks these features; a separate MechaMeme grant is not required. The app obtains its provider configuration automatically. No API-key entry, server URL or PC connection is needed.

If access has just changed, tap **Refresh access**. Free users can continue importing, cropping, editing, browsing, saving and sharing. Internet is required for sign-in, Pro verification and AI requests.

## Templates

Open the meme-style picker to browse formats, inspect examples, choose a style or create a custom template. Auto template lets the AI select a format. Template drafts and changes are saved locally.

## Zoom and navigation

Swipe left or right to change takes and up or down to switch photos. Tap the take indicator or pull it down to open the take drawer. Release after a deliberate pull to keep it open; short pulls dismiss the preview. Swipe up from the drawer header or bottom handle to close it. Takes, Library and Settings open in matching panels beneath the top controls. Pinch to zoom the existing framed image, then drag to pan. Android Back resets inline zoom. Double-tap opens the full-screen viewer. In a short window, **All controls** exposes the actions.

## Storage and interrupted requests

Photos, kept takes and template changes are local, not a cloud backup. Export anything important before clearing app data or changing devices.

The app manages requests and repeated submissions. Stopping the wait may not cancel a provider request already running. Closing the app during generation can lose an in-flight result; the app will not silently purchase the same take again on reopening. Retrying an interrupted request can incur another provider charge. Provider configuration and billing are managed by the RykerSoft administrator.

## Name or delete photos

Open **Library** to browse original photos in a grid. Each corner badge shows the number of takes. Tap an image to select it, or hold it to open its action menu. Choose **Rename** to change its name, or **Delete** to remove the photo and its takes. Names stay on this device. Deleting requires confirmation. With a keyboard, focus a photo and press F2; right-click also opens these actions.
