# MechaMeme

MechaMeme turns your photos into editable memes in a playful Android workspace, with a public Community feed and a private on-device library.

- Generate and revise captions for free, with optional meme styles and directions.
- Import camera or gallery photos, crop locally, and edit caption text and placement.
- Browse Community, or scroll your originals and generations in My Library.
- Swipe across a library image to compare versions; dots mark the selected version.
- Bookmark private favorites, save PNGs, or send an image through Android sharing.
- Sign in with Google and thumbs-up an image to publish a snapshot to Community.
- Create custom templates and use free template assistance.

## PRO Features

* **Recreate me** generates a new scene based on your photo and requires RykerSoft Pro.

Use the same Google account as RykerSoft. An active Pro grant in any RykerSoft app unlocks Recreate me. Captions, editing and Community publication do not require Pro. Publishing requires Google sign-in.

Your library stays on-device; it is not a cloud backup or separated by Google account. Only images you explicitly publish appear in Community. Public images can be downloaded by anyone. AI actions send selected content to the configured service/provider; no PC or manual API-key setup is required.
