# MechaMeme updates

## 1.0.31 — November 3, 2026

- Now on Windows and Linux. Sign in with Google opens your web browser.
- Library opens fullscreen, with caption editing integrated into the image workspace.
- Faster image handoffs and swipe previews keep complete cards and captions visible.
- Brighter stack position indicators and steadier feed card angles.
- Home offers a direct editing shortcut for private library images.

## 1.0.30 — November 2, 2026

- Captioned Community posts carry their reusable image, examples and template instructions. Add to library keeps them together for later use.
- Remixes stay private in My Library; repeated remixes or matching existing images become new variations.
- PNG exports include editable meme data. Library photo actions offer Save editable meme (.meme); importing either restores captions, layout, guidance, examples and known attribution privately.
- Portable files include the renderer font/license and have a documented versioned format and agent CLI. Other apps may strip PNG metadata; use .meme for dependable transfers.
- Smaller square and wide images open in compact, centered Community popups. Large emoji lists wrap and scroll while controls stay reachable.

## 1.0.29 — November 1, 2026

- Publishing starts a Community image with your thumbs-up; you can remove your vote, including on your own post.
- Posts disappear from Community when their thumbs-up count reaches zero.
- Compact centered username badge and a tighter gap above the full image in picture view.
- Raised arcade-style actions, clear frame spacing, responsive landscape layout and zoom reset.
- Close picture view with Android Back, Escape or a tap outside; the close X is removed.

## 1.0.28 — November 1, 2026

- Upvote other users’ Community posts, with one reversible vote per Google account and public counts.
- Separate icon-only Add to library action in the feed and picture view, with an accessible label and saved checkmark.
- Save a private copy without leaving Community or signing in; existing copies prevent duplicate additions.
- Keep one-tap Remix for opening the studio directly.
- Responsive action rows keep all controls reachable on narrow phones.

## 1.0.27 — November 1, 2026

- Prominent Remix action on Community posts and picture view.
- One tap opens a private studio copy, ready for captions or generation, without sign-in or import steps.
- Remix copies retain source attribution locally and stay private until explicitly published.
- Loading feedback and retryable errors when a public image cannot be opened.
- Roomier author and action rows on narrow phone screens.

## 1.0.26 — November 1, 2026

- Choose a unique public username in Settings; Google account names and emails stay private.
- Delete owned posts directly from Community or picture view, even after deleting the local library copy.
- Explicit delete confirmation, retryable errors, and clear session/ownership errors.
- Expanded emoji reactions, bundled classic template photos, and vibrant retro-space themes with directional gradients.
- Smoother library rendering and gestures; existing photos, takes, settings and signing identity are preserved.

## 1.0.24 — November 1, 2026

- Public Community feed with Google-authenticated publication and owner removal; private My Library with sorting and all originals/takes.
- Free caption generation, revisions and template assistance; only Recreate me requires Pro.
- Image-led scrolling Home, horizontal take swipes, pinch zoom and a draggable Add photo button.
- Compact retro header with collapsible filters and scroll-aware visibility.
- Dates above images on the right, usernames below Community images on the left, and visual take indicators.
- Share, Save, Thumbs up and Keep actions ordered consistently in the feed.
- More stable thumbnail rendering and caption-hold layers while scrolling.

## 1.0.23 — November 1, 2026

- Your Photos is now Library: a two-column grid of original images with number-only version badges.
- Cinematic thumbnail-to-photo selection transitions, with reduced-motion fades.
- Takes, Library and Settings share anchored panels, consistent headers, dimming and reveal animation.
- Pull down from the take indicator to reveal its drawer. Placement stays fixed on release and the purple backdrop follows the gesture.
- Swipe up from the take drawer header or bottom handle to close it.
- Deleting a selected take keeps the same numbered slot selected; deleting the last take selects the previous one.

## 1.0.22 — November 1, 2026

- Add captions manually to an imported photo with the pencil button, without AI generation or Pro.
- Save as new take keeps the original photo and selects the new captioned version.
- Saved manual takes persist after reopening the app; leaving an unfinished draft discards it.

## 1.0.21 — November 1, 2026

- Meme God is now MechaMeme, with a new red geometric launcher icon and splash artwork. Existing photos, settings and Pro access are retained.
- Static, crisp controls with layered coral, teal and violet backing plates and soft shadows.
- Desaturated mosaic processing effect; caption transitions remain and smoke is removed.
- Long-press a photo in Your Photos for a Rename/Delete menu. Names persist locally; deletion asks for confirmation.
- Refined caption editing, layout selection, image cropping and multiple-caption controls.
- Provider/model selection, voice direction input, and Android image-sharing intake from the updates since the first public release.

## 1.0.0 — October 31, 2026

- First public RykerSoft release.
- Free photo editing, cropping, local history, keeping, export and sharing.
- Automatic AI configuration for users with RykerSoft Pro access.
- Device-direct captions, photo recreations and template assistance.
- Pinch zoom and panning on the existing framed image.
- Reduced gap between the photo and its action buttons.
- Production signing and Android 9+ support.
