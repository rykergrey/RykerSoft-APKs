## Getting started
Choose **Create your first comic** or **New comic**. Describe your idea, or choose a text starter, then pick a style from the text menu. **Reference images** and **Fine-tune** are optional: expand them to add images, variations, themes, or tone. Choose **Generate comic · Pro** when ready.

Your idea, settings, and reference images save on this device as you change them. Home offers **Continue draft** after leaving or reopening the app. Check the save status before closing the app; if storage fails, keep the workbench open and choose **Retry saving**.

## Your workbench
Saved sessions open to their comics. Choose **Create another comic** to reveal creation controls beside the artwork on desktop or above it on a phone. You can hide these controls again. Generation continues when you return Home within the app, and Home shows work in progress. Keep the app running for requests to finish; closing or reloading it interrupts requests. Saved scripts and completed artwork remain available, with missing panels ready to retry.

## Editing and sharing
Choose **Edit caption** to change words and fonts. Choose **Edit image** to describe an artwork change. Panel **More** menus contain duplication, moving, creating adjacent panels, individual image exports, and deletion. Comics can have up to three panels through these creation controls. Image history offers previous and next version buttons.

**Export comic** offers **Comic with captions**, **Images only**, and an **Editable backup (.zip)**. Complete missing artwork before exporting the whole illustrated comic. An editable backup can include unfinished artwork. Android opens its share sheet; web browsers download the file.

## Library and recovery
Search session names, comic titles, ideas, or styles. Sort by newest, oldest, or name. **Rename** changes a session's name without changing individual comic titles.

Deleted panels offer **Undo deletion**, including after reopening their session. **Comic actions → Move comic to Trash** removes a whole comic. Home also offers **Move to Trash** for an entire session. Open **Library options → Trash** to restore deleted comics or sessions later. Deletion does not permanently erase this work.

## Backups
Choose **Back up session** in the workbench to export every comic in the session. From Home, choose **Library options → Import backup (.zip)**. Imports create a new session with new IDs so they cannot overwrite existing comics. Backups retain captions, styles, artwork history, and recoverable deleted panels.

Keep backups outside the app. Uninstalling or clearing app data can erase the local library, drafts, and Trash.

## Desktop installation
On Windows, run the portable `.exe`. On Linux, make the AppImage executable and run it, or extract the `.tar.gz` archive and launch `comiccrafting`. Keep the extracted Linux runtime files together. Your drafts and comics are stored in the application's local user profile, separately from other devices. Use backups to move work between them.

Google sign-in on desktop opens your system browser. Complete sign-in there, then return to ComicCraft.ing. Keep the application running until sign-in completes.

## PRO Features
Items marked * require ComicCraft.ing Pro access for your RykerSoft account and an internet connection.

* **Comic generation** — Create a comic from an idea or reference image.
* **Image editing and retry** — Change artwork or retry a missing panel.
* **Adjacent panels** — Extend a scene with a new panel.
* **Caption improvements** — Use AI to improve wording.

Saved comics, manual caption editing, panel organization, recovery, and backups remain available without Pro.

Open **RykerSoft account**, sign in with Google, and choose **Refresh access** after your administrator enables this app. Another app’s Pro access does not unlock ComicCraft.ing. Sign-out disables AI access while leaving saved work available.

## Troubleshooting
If access cannot be verified, check your connection and refresh access. If Pro is active but Gemini is not configured, ask your RykerSoft administrator to configure this app’s Gemini service.

A failed panel retains its caption and successful artwork elsewhere. Choose **Retry this panel** to request only its artwork. Failed image edits keep the original image. Failed adjacent-panel creation keeps the proposed caption and scene instructions for retry.
