## Getting started
Tap **Start Crafting**. Enter a premise or upload a reference image, then choose a visual style. Suggested themes and tone are optional starting points. Tap **Generate Comic · Pro** to create a comic. Completed comics are saved on this device and grouped into sessions.

## Editing and sharing
Tap a caption to edit text or change its font. Use panel controls to duplicate or reorder panels. Image history lets you revisit earlier artwork. Export a picture for sharing, a complete strip, or a ZIP backup. Android opens its share sheet; choose an installed app or file destination. Web browsers download the file.

## Backups
Use the archive button to export a session as a ZIP file. From Home, choose **Import Backup** to restore one. Keep backups outside the app; uninstalling or clearing app data can erase your local library. Imports with matching comic IDs update those comics.

## PRO Features
Items marked * require ComicCraft.ing Pro access, managed for your RykerSoft account.

* **Generate comics** from a premise or reference image.
* **Transform artwork** by describing the change.
* **Generate related panels** before or after existing artwork.
* **Enhance captions** using the chosen style and tone.

Open **RykerSoft account**, sign in with Google, and choose **Refresh access** after your administrator enables this app. Another app’s Pro access does not unlock ComicCraft.ing. No separate paid APK or unlock code is needed. Sign-out disables AI access while leaving saved comics available.

## Troubleshooting
If access cannot be verified, check your internet connection and refresh it. If Pro is active but Gemini is not configured, ask your RykerSoft administrator to configure this app’s Gemini service. A partially illustrated comic keeps its script; retry generation when the service is available. Keep the app open while it creates artwork.
